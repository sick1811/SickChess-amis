# SickChess – friends version

**English** | [Français](README-fr.md)

A chess game for Windows: play against the computer (Stockfish 17.1, or Maia 3, which plays like a human, depending on the level), with two players on the same PC or online with Steam friends, plus puzzles, opening training and analysis of your games.

- **Maia 3, two models:** the small one (5M, 21 MB, included with the game) and the large one (79M, 312 MB, the most human). The game uses the large one as soon as it is available, except on a low-end PC, which keeps the small one. [Maia-3 by the CSSLab, University of Toronto](https://github.com/CSSLab/maia3).
- **Syzygy endgame tablebases:** all endgames with 3 to 5 pieces (940 MB), for evaluation, endgames and learning.

**Install:** download [SickChess_Installation.exe](https://github.com/sick1811/SickChess-amis/releases/latest/download/SickChess_Installation.exe), run it, then open SickChess from the desktop icon.

- You need an Internet connection and about 2 GB of free space: the puzzles (220 MB), the endgame tablebases (940 MB) and the large Maia model (312 MB) are downloaded during installation (about 1.5 GB, one time only).
- No administrator rights are needed. Windows asks once for firewall permission: answering Yes allows updates and online play.
- If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC": click "More info", then "Run anyway" (the game is not signed yet).

After that, the game updates itself from the menu, never during a game. Your games, profile and settings are kept.

**Play online:** Steam must be running. Open SickChess **before** accepting a friend's invitation, or type the lobby code. Steam shows "Spacewar": this is normal, it is Valve's test number until the game is released on Steam.
